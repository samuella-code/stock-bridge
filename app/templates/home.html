Expenses & profit

Sales tell part of the story.

See what remains after the cost of goods sold and recorded expenses. StockBridge uses the recorded unit costs on sales to calculate COGS.

Sales revenue₦85,000
− Cost of goods sold₦50,000
− Recorded expenses₦10,000
= Net profit₦25,000
Illustrative example, consistent with StockBridge reports.

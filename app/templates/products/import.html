Import Products

Add many products at once from a spreadsheet, product list or text file.

Review and edit every product before importing. New products do not need to exist in any catalogue. Product Name is required; missing quantities, prices and thresholds default to zero, and Unit defaults to “unit”. Up to 1,000 rows or text lines and 2 MB per upload.

Upload Spreadsheet

Import products from an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV (.csv) file.

Include a Product Name column. Optional headings: Opening Quantity, Cost Price, Selling Price, Category, Unit, Low Stock Threshold, SKU and Description.

Use one worksheet and plain values without formulas. Format SKU as Text to preserve leading zeros. Existing products are never overwritten.

Paste Product List

Copy a product list from Notes, WhatsApp, Word or another app and paste it here.

One product per line. Optional format: Product Name - Opening Quantity - Cost Price - Selling Price. Use spaced hyphens, commas or tabs. Copied tables can include template headers (Quantity also works). Ambiguous lines remain editable names.

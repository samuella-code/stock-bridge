Revenue: Money earned from completed sales. Cost of goods sold (COGS): The cost saved for the products you sold.

Profit calculations: Gross profit is revenue minus the cost saved when each item was sold. Net profit is gross profit minus recorded operating expenses. Inventory purchases are tracked separately; they are not deducted a second time. Inventory value uses current stock × the latest recorded unit cost.

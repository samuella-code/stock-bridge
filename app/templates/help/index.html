Getting started

Create your account, verify your email and review your business details. Add the products you sell and their existing quantities as opening stock. The dashboard checklist recommends next steps for the selected business.

Record only genuine sales, restocks and expenses. You do not need to invent transactions to complete setup.

Open dashboard

Creating and managing products

Enter a clear product name, unit, purchase cost, selling price and low-stock threshold. Search and filter the catalogue to find products; use product details to review their history. Mark products inactive when appropriate rather than discarding history.

Open Products

Opening stock and inventory

Opening stock is the quantity you already have when registering a product. Sales reduce stock; restocking increases it. Review low-stock and out-of-stock indicators in Inventory. After registration, correct quantities through stock adjustments instead of editing current stock directly.

Open Inventory

Recording sales

Search for a product, add it to the basket, enter the quantity and review the selling price. Add further products to the same sale, then review the date, payment method and total before submitting once. JavaScript is required for the basket. Available stock limits the quantities you can sell.

Open Sales

Restocking products

Record goods received for resale. Add the received products to the basket, enter quantities and purchase costs per unit, and include supplier details or notes where useful. Review the basket before submitting once. Restocking history preserves purchase costs.

Open Restocking

Stock adjustments

Open a product’s details and choose its stock adjustment action. Select an increase or decrease and enter a genuine reason, such as damage, loss, returns or a correction. Review the quantity and notes before confirming. Adjustments must not make stock negative and remain in stock history.

Choose a product in Inventory

Recording expenses

Record operating costs such as rent, electricity and transport with the correct amount, category and date. Goods bought for resale belong in Restocking. Review expense history before adding an expense again.

Open Expenses

Dashboard and reports

Choose Today, Week, Month or Custom and select Apply. For Custom, enter both dates first. Dashboard summary cards follow the selected period; the revenue chart shows the last seven days with a readable daily breakdown.

Sales revenue is not profit. Product costs and operating expenses affect the reported profit. Review the reporting period and transaction history when figures need explanation.

Open Reports

After an interrupted submission

If saving times out or the response is lost, check the relevant transaction history before recording anything again. A retry using the same submission reference and unchanged contents is protected against duplication. Changing contents under an already used reference is rejected.

If the form expires or the outcome is uncertain, do not immediately create a fresh transaction. Confirm the original outcome in history first. Refresh the form for a genuinely new transaction only after checking. Never share submission tokens or account credentials.

Managing multiple businesses

Where your account plan permits it, use My Businesses to manage businesses and the Working in selector to choose the active one. Basic supports one business; Plus supports up to two. Each business keeps separate records. Confirm the active business before recording activity.

Open My Businesses

How to read these figures

Revenue = quantity × saved sale price. COGS = quantity × saved sale-time cost. Estimated gross profit = revenue − COGS. Estimated result after recorded expenses = gross profit − recorded operating expenses. Voided sales and expenses are excluded. Stock purchases are separate and are not deducted again.

Compare the recorded changes to identify which businesses need a closer review of sales records, saved costs or expense receipts. These comparisons do not establish causes or predict future performance.

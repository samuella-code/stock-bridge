How these numbers work

Revenue uses saved sale prices. The cost of goods sold uses the cost saved when each item was sold. Gross profit is revenue minus those costs. What you kept is estimated gross profit minus recorded running costs. Voided sales and expenses are excluded. Stock purchases are tracked separately.

The stock review uses its own 30 complete reporting days, from {{ insight.stock.start.strftime('%d/%m/%Y') }} up to the start of {{ insight.stock.end.strftime('%d/%m/%Y') }}. Reporting days use a 1:00 am WAT cutoff. The list shows up to 10 active products added by the start of that window that still have stock. Use it to review your records and stock counts.

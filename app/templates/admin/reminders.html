Reminders and win-back messages

What the two buttons on the Notifications page do, who gets them and when.

← Notifications

Trial reminder

Who
Account owners whose trial is ending or ended and who have not chosen a plan. Never employees, suspended accounts or people reminded in the last 7 days.
When
Automatic: day 5 of the trial, the day it ends, and 3 days after. Or send one by hand.
Says
Their records are safe, they can still view them, and how to pick a plan.
Goal
Turn a finished trial into a paying plan.

Win-back message

Who
Owners who turned off renewal, or whose paid access just ended.
When
Automatic: 7 days before paid access ends, on the day, and 3 days after. Stops when they pay or turn renewal back on.
Says
Their access end date, that records are kept, a link to billing, and an optional “Why did you stop?” question.
Goal
Keep the customer, or learn why they left.

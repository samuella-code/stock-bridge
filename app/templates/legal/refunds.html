Stopping subscription renewal

When recurring subscriptions are enabled, open Billing & Plan, find the subscription and choose Cancel renewal, confirming the action. Successful cancellation stops renewal; already-paid access continues until the displayed paid-through timestamp. A scheduled subscription can be cancelled before its start. If a provider operation remains pending or cancellation fails, check billing status and request assistance rather than assuming renewal has stopped.

After paid access ends

Records are retained. Paid mutations are restricted after entitlement expiry according to the applicable access rules. When Plus ends, customers with existing account access fall back to that entitlement. With two businesses and a one-business allowance, an explicit active-business selection is required; the other business remains stored but locked.

Renewals and plan changes

Authorized recurring plans renew on their configured monthly or yearly schedule unless renewal is stopped. Review the amount, interval and status shown in Billing & Plan. See Subscription Terms for plan-change timing.

Refund eligibility — decision pending

The application does not establish an approved refund window or automatic refund process. The owner must approve eligibility, windows, exceptional circumstances, handling of renewals and duplicate/incorrect charges, and the request/review process. This draft promises neither a refund nor a no-refund policy. Cancellation itself does not automatically issue a refund.

Billing questions and disputes

Keep the payment reference and describe the issue; never send passwords, full card details or security codes. Failed, unknown or unverified payment outcomes do not by themselves establish paid entitlement. Avoid repeated checkout while a payment is unresolved.

Decision pending: operational billing/refund contact, evidence requirements, dispute escalation and response times. See Contact & Support. Statutory rights are subject to applicable law and legal review.

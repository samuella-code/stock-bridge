Seven-day trial, when enabled

An eligible new customer account starts one seven-day trial after email verification, including verified Google signup. No card is required and no automatic charge follows trial expiry. Returning sign-in, logout/login and Google login do not restart a trial. Trial access supports one business. Accounts with existing paid access do not receive a replacement trial.

Payment and renewal

Review the server-selected plan, amount and monthly/yearly interval before authorizing Paystack payment. Paid access requires verified provider outcomes. Recurring subscriptions renew according to the authorized provider schedule unless renewal is stopped. A failed payment does not create a newly paid period; previously verified paid access remains until its end. Unresolved payments require status review before retrying.

Upgrades, downgrades and interval changes

Basic-to-Plus upgrades with the same interval use an immediate, new full-price checkout. Other supported plan/interval changes are scheduled at the existing paid-period end and may require reusable payment authorization. No custom proration or automatic unused-period refund is implemented. Review the confirmation screen before proceeding; if scheduling is unavailable, cancel renewal and choose a new plan after the paid period.

Plus supports up to two businesses with separate records. On return to a one-business entitlement, customers with two businesses must explicitly select the accessible business. The restricted business remains stored and can become available again when Plus is restored; it is not recreated.

Expiry, cancellation and existing access protection

Trial or subscription expiry retains records and restricts new paid activity according to access rules. Cancelling renewal preserves already-paid access. Customers with existing valid account access retain core one-business access and are not converted to Basic. They may voluntarily obtain Plus; when Plus ends, their existing entitlement resumes.

For refunds and cancellation details see Refund & Cancellation Policy. Future price-change notice and the scope/duration of historical payment obligations require owner and legal review; this draft does not change existing entitlements.

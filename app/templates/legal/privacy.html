Information used by StockBridge

Account records include your name, email, a password hash for email authentication, verification status and account status. Google sign-in stores the provider identity associated with your account; it does not give StockBridge your Google password.

Business records include business names and optional logos, products, inventory quantities and movements, prices and costs, sales and sale items, restocks, expenses, and report inputs. Notification history, business-specific preferences and email delivery jobs are also stored.

Subscription and payment records include payment references, amounts, currency, verification outcomes, plan and interval, subscription dates and provider identifiers. Paystack handles payment authorization; provider authorization metadata may be retained for renewals. StockBridge does not collect full card numbers or card security codes through its forms.

Necessary session cookies support sign-in and request protection. Operational logs may contain request timing, route, error and account-related audit information. The public site requests fonts from Google Fonts, which receives browser requests. No advertising analytics integration was identified in the reviewed application.

How information is used

Information supports authentication, inventory and business management, reports, access controls, payment verification, customer support and service reliability. Verification and password-reset emails are essential account communications. Optional inventory emails and activity notifications follow the saved preferences for the relevant business.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts the application and processes requests and runtime logs.
  • Neon PostgreSQL stores hosted application records.
  • Cloudflare R2 stores private business logo objects through S3-compatible storage.
  • Paystack processes StockBridge access/subscription payments when enabled.
  • The configured SMTP provider delivers transactional and inventory emails.
  • Google supports optional Google authentication and public font delivery.

Providers may process information outside Nigeria. Decision pending: identify the legal operator, SMTP provider, processing locations, applicable lawful bases and international-transfer safeguards with counsel. No compliance certification is claimed.

Customer choices, retention and requests

Account and business authorization checks restrict access to owned records. These controls are not a guarantee that all security incidents can be prevented. Customers can edit supported profile/product fields and manage optional business notification preferences.

Under applicable Nigerian data-protection law, rights may include access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, subject to legal conditions. You may raise concerns with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. See the Nigeria Data Protection Act reference.

Cancellation or entitlement expiry does not automatically delete business records. This application does not provide automatic account deletion or a complete personal-data export workflow. Requests require identity verification and an approved support process; legally required billing records and backup copies may have retention limitations.

Decision pending: retention periods, backup deletion handling, request procedure and response commitments. An approved privacy contact must be supplied on Contact & Support before public release.

What to include in a request

  • General support: describe the page and action involved, with a redacted screenshot if useful.
  • Account access: describe sign-in or verification difficulties; never share a password or verification/reset token.

  • Privacy requests: describe the access, correction or deletion request. Identity verification and retention limits will apply through the approved process.

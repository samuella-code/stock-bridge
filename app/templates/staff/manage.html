How Pro affects staff access

Continuous paid Pro renewals keep access in place. If Pro expires or you downgrade, staff access stops. After returning to Pro, restore each employee’s access yourself. Removed employees need a new invitation.

Roles and permissions

Choose a shared set of permissions for each role. Editing a role changes it for every employee and pending invitation using that role. Names and duty labels are descriptions, not permissions.

Permission limits and a Restocking-only example

Billing, subscriptions, voids and manual stock adjustments stay owner-only. Only you can grant team-administration or export authority. Use Team management to grant these authorities to a shared role; exports remain disabled.

New Manager roles include financial-report access; other starting roles do not. Owners can edit each role. Editing product details does not allow stock adjustments or unrestricted financial access. Restocking receipts are limited to assigned businesses; they do not include financial reports or exports.

For a Restocking-only role, select View products and stock, Record restocking, and View restocking receipts and history. Leave product creation, product editing, sales, expenses and financial reports unchecked.
